How to Play Mini Chess

You control the white pieces and the computer controls the black pieces. Click one of your pieces to see its possible moves.

Click a highlighted square to move your piece. Capture the computer's king to win the game.

Mini Chess Controls

  • ♟️ Click a white piece to select it.
  • 🟢 Green squares show possible moves.
  • ⚔️ Red squares contain capturable pieces.
  • 🤖 The computer moves automatically.

Tips

  • 🧠 Plan your moves carefully.
  • 🎯 Look for pieces that you can capture.
  • 🛡️ Protect your important pieces.
  • 🏆 Capture the black king to win.
