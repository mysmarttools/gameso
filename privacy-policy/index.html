Privacy Policy

Learn how Gameso may collect and use information, how cookies and advertising services work, and what privacy choices may be available to you.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Gameso. We respect the privacy of our visitors and aim to handle information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit Gameso.

The information and practices described below depend on the features, hosting arrangements, and third-party services actually used on our website. Where applicable law requires consent, we will seek it as required.

1. Information We May Collect

Information You Provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, message, and any other information you choose to include. We use this information to review and respond to your inquiry.

Technical Information

Our hosting provider or security services may process technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages requested, and access times. The precise information collected depends on the services configured for the website.

Usage Information

If analytics tools are enabled, they may collect information about how visitors interact with pages and games. This information may help us understand website performance and identify areas for improvement.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and secure Gameso.
  • Respond to visitor messages and support requests.
  • Identify broken links and technical problems.
  • Understand website usage where analytics are enabled.
  • Deliver and measure advertising where advertising services are enabled.
  • Comply with applicable laws and protect our rights.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored by a website or service in your browser. Websites and third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics, preferences, and advertising.

The technologies used on Gameso depend on the services currently enabled. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect the operation of certain website features.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

Gameso may use Google AdSense or other advertising services if they are enabled on the website. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity, and measure advertising performance.

Depending on your location, consent choices, and available settings, Google and its partners may use information about visits to this and other websites to show personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

The actual data processing depends on which advertising services are implemented and how they are configured. We do not claim that a service is active unless it has actually been enabled.

Learn more by visiting Google's advertising information and Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Third-Party Games and Services

Some games may be hosted by or embedded from third-party providers. When you interact with these games or follow external links, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

Gameso does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage visitors to review the relevant policies before using external services.

6. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information as a standalone mailing list. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate, secure, host, or maintain the website. Advertising or analytics providers may also process data when their services are enabled.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, or the safety of users and others.

7. Data Retention and Security

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, including support, security, operational, or legal needs. Retention periods may vary depending on the service involved.

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or online storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Gameso offers online games that may appeal to a broad audience. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps where necessary.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also be able to manage advertising and cookie preferences through available settings.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

10. International Data Processing

Our hosting provider or third-party services may process information in countries other than your own. Where required by law, appropriate safeguards will apply to such processing.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, advertising arrangements, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
