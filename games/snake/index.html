🎮 How to Play Snake Game

The objective of Snake is simple: guide the snake towards the food that appears on the game board. Every time the snake collects food, it grows longer and your score increases. As the snake gets longer, you need to plan your movements more carefully.

  1. Start the game: Click the Start Game button to begin playing.
  2. Move your snake: Use the arrow keys on your computer or the on-screen direction buttons on your mobile device.
  3. Collect food: Guide the snake towards the food to increase your score.
  4. Avoid collisions: Stay away from the walls and your snake's own body.
  5. Beat your best score: Keep practising and try to achieve a higher score in your next round.

⭐ 7 Tips to Get a Higher Snake Game Score

Want to survive longer and improve your score? Use these practical tips during your next game.

  1. Plan your next move: Look at the open space around your snake before changing direction.
  2. Avoid sudden turns: Think ahead instead of changing direction without considering the space available.
  3. Keep enough room to move: Try not to trap your snake in a small area as it grows.
  4. Watch your snake's body: Remember that every piece of the growing body creates another obstacle.
  5. Do not chase food blindly: Choose a safe route to the food instead of taking a risky shortcut.
  6. Practise controlled movements: Learning to make accurate turns can help you avoid unnecessary collisions.
  7. Learn from each round: Notice what caused your previous game to end and try a different approach next time.
