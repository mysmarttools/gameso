How to Play Snake Game

Control the snake and collect the food that appears on the game board. Every time the snake eats food, it grows longer and your score increases.

Avoid hitting the walls and don't let the snake hit its own body. Try to get the highest score possible!

Snake Game Controls

  • 📱 Use the direction buttons on mobile.
  • 🎮 Collect food to increase your score.
  • 🏆 Try to beat your best score.
