How to Play Mini Chess

You play as White and the computer plays as Black. Select one of your pieces and then choose a valid square to move it.

Capture the computer's pieces and try to capture its king before the computer captures yours.

Mini Chess Controls

  • ♟️ Click one of your chess pieces.
  • 👆 Select a highlighted destination.
  • ⚔️ Capture opponent pieces.
  • 🏆 Capture the enemy king to win.
