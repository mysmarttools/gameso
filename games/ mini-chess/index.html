Mini Chess Board Game

Mini Chess is a simple online board game inspired by classic chess. Select your piece and then choose a highlighted square to move. Capture the opponent's pieces and capture the king to win.

How to Play

  • ♟️ Click or tap your chess piece.
  • 🟢 Green squares show possible moves.
  • 🔴 Red squares show captures.
  • 🏆 Capture the opponent king to win.
  • 🔄 Use Restart Game to start again.
