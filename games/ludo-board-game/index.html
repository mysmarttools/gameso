🎯 How to Play Ludo Online

Enjoy this classic Ludo board game online at Gameso. Play against the computer or take turns with friends on the same device. Move your coloured tokens around the board and try to bring all four home before your opponents.

🎲 Roll a Six

Your token starts in its coloured base. Roll a 6 to move it onto the track.

🏃 Move Your Tokens

After rolling, select a highlighted token. It moves forward by the number shown on the dice.

🏆 Reach Home

Complete the circuit, enter your coloured home lane and roll the exact number to finish.

📖 Ludo Rules

  • Players take turns rolling one six-sided die.
  • A six is required to bring a token out of the base.
  • Rolling a six gives you another roll.
  • Landing on an opponent's token on an unprotected track square sends that token back to its base.
  • Starting squares and star squares are treated as safe spots in this version.
  • You must roll the exact number needed to reach the finish.
  • The first player to bring all four tokens home wins.
