How to Play Ludo

Ludo is a classic board game played by two to four players. Each player has four tokens. Roll the dice and move your tokens around the board. The goal is to safely move all four tokens to the center before your opponents.

Ludo Board Game Rules

  • 🎲 Roll the dice to move your tokens.
  • 🏠 Each player starts with four tokens.
  • 🚀 A six can allow a token to enter the board.
  • 🎯 Move your tokens around the board.
  • 🏆 Get all four tokens to the center to win.
