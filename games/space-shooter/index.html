How to Play Space Shooter

Control your spaceship at the bottom of the screen. Shoot the incoming enemy ships and avoid getting hit. The longer you survive, the higher your score becomes.

Destroy enemies to earn points and reach higher levels as the game becomes faster and harder.

Space Shooter Controls

  • ⬅️ Left Arrow — Move spaceship left.
  • ➡️ Right Arrow — Move spaceship right.
  • 🔫 Space — Shoot.
  • 📱 Use the touch buttons on mobile.

Tips

  • 🎯 Aim carefully at enemy ships.
  • ⚡ Keep moving to avoid enemy attacks.
  • ❤️ Protect your lives.
  • 🏆 Try to beat your highest score.
