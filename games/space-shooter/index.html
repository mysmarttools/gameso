How to Play Space Shooter

Your mission is to protect your ship while enemy spacecraft descend from the top of the screen. Fire continuously, avoid enemy attacks, and clear each wave to face a greater challenge.

  1. Click Start Mission to begin.
  2. Move your ship with the Left and Right arrow keys or A and D.
  3. Hold Space to fire laser shots at enemy ships.
  4. On mobile, drag your finger across the play area to steer. The ship fires automatically while you play.
  5. Destroy enemies to earn points and advance through waves.
  6. Collect falling power-ups for extra firepower or a temporary shield.
  7. Avoid enemy projectiles and collisions. You start with three lives.

Tips for a Higher Score

  • Keep moving: A moving ship is harder for enemies to hit.
  • Watch incoming fire: Change direction early rather than waiting until a projectile is close.
  • Collect power-ups: Useful upgrades can make difficult waves easier.
  • Stay away from corners: Leave room to dodge attacks on either side.
  • Clear each wave: Destroy the remaining enemies to unlock the next wave.
Mission tip: Don't stay in one position while shooting. Move across the field, watch enemy patterns, and leave yourself space to escape incoming projectiles.

Space Shooter – Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play Space Shooter online for free?

Yes. This Gameso version runs in a modern web browser without requiring a separate download or paid game installation.

How do I control the spaceship?

Use the Left and Right arrow keys or A and D on a keyboard. On a phone or tablet, drag across the game area to move your ship.

How do I shoot enemy ships?

Hold the Space key on a computer. On mobile devices, use the FIRE button. Touchscreen play also supports automatic firing while you move.

How many lives do I get?

You begin with three lives. Enemy collisions and incoming projectiles can cost a life, so keep moving and watch the battlefield.

What do power-ups do?

Some defeated enemies drop collectible items. A weapon upgrade temporarily increases your firing power, while a shield pickup protects you from damage for a limited time.

Does the game become harder?

Yes. New waves introduce more enemies and faster action, making movement and timing increasingly important.

Is my high score saved?

The game attempts to store your best score in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data or disabling storage may remove the saved score.
