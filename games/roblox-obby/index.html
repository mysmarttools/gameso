🕹️ How to Play Battle Arena

Your goal is to survive while eliminating as many enemies as possible. Start the game and use movement controls to avoid contact with enemies. Aim carefully and shoot before they reach you.

⌨️ PC and Laptop Controls

  • W, A, S, D: Move up, left, down and right.
  • Arrow keys: Move around the arena.
  • Mouse: Aim in the direction of your cursor.
  • Left mouse button: Shoot at enemies.
  • Spacebar: Shoot.
  • Restart Game: Begin a new round at any time.

📱 Mobile Controls

  • Tap the directional arrows to move your character.
  • Press and hold the SHOOT button to fire.
  • Watch your health and keep moving to avoid enemies.

💡 Tips to Get a Higher Score

  • Keep moving: Staying in one place makes it easier for enemies to reach you.
  • Aim ahead: Point toward the direction an enemy is moving.
  • Watch your health: Avoid close contact with enemies whenever possible.
  • Use the full arena: Move around the open space to create distance.
  • Shoot consistently: Keep firing when enemies are within range.
  • Practice your controls: Smooth movement and aiming can help you survive longer.
