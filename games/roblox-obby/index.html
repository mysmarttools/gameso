🎮 How to Play Roblox Obby

Welcome to Gameso's Roblox Obby-style platform challenge! Move your character across floating platforms, jump over dangerous lava and reach the green finish platform to complete each level.

🕹️ Game Controls

  • Move left: Left Arrow or A key.
  • Move right: Right Arrow or D key.
  • Jump: Spacebar, Up Arrow or W key.
  • Mobile: Use the on-screen movement and jump buttons.
  • Restart: Restart your current level at any time.

🏁 Tips to Complete the Obby

Watch the gaps between platforms before jumping. Land safely on checkpoint platforms to save your progress, avoid red lava zones and build momentum for longer jumps. Reach the finish platform to unlock the next stage.

❓ Is Roblox Obby Free to Play?

This original browser-based obstacle-course game is free to play on Gameso. It is inspired by the obstacle-course genre popular on Roblox, but it is not the official Roblox game and does not require a Roblox account.
