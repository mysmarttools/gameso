What Is Minesweeper?

Minesweeper is a classic logic and puzzle game where players uncover squares on a grid while avoiding hidden mines. Each safe square may reveal a number showing how many mines touch it, including diagonally. Use these clues to work out which squares are safe and which should be marked with a flag.

This free Minesweeper online game runs directly in your browser. There is no installation or account required. Play on a computer, tablet, or compatible mobile device, and choose an easier or more challenging board whenever you want.

How to Play Minesweeper

  1. Start the game: Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard and open a square. Your first square is always safe.
  2. Read the numbers: A number shows how many mines are in the eight neighboring squares.
  3. Mark hidden mines: Use right-click on a computer or turn on Flag Mode on a phone to mark suspected mines.
  4. Find safe squares: Use number clues and flags to determine which unopened squares can be revealed safely.
  5. Clear the board: Reveal every square that does not contain a mine to win.
💡 Beginner tip: If a revealed square shows 1 and only one of its neighboring squares remains unopened, that square must contain a mine. Use clues like this to solve the board logically.

Minesweeper Difficulty Levels

Easy Minesweeper

The Easy board is 9 × 9 with 10 mines. It is ideal for beginners who want to learn number clues, practise flag placement, and build confidence.

Medium Minesweeper

The Medium board is 16 × 16 with 40 mines. More squares and clues create a bigger challenge, making observation and planning more important.

Hard Minesweeper

The Hard board is 30 columns wide and 16 rows tall, with 99 mines. It rewards patience, logical deduction, and accurate flag placement.

Minesweeper Tips and Strategies

  • Study every number: Each number is a clue about the surrounding eight squares.
  • Use flags carefully: Flag a square when the available clues support your decision.
  • Look for overlapping clues: Nearby numbers often help identify safe squares.
  • Explore empty areas: Squares with no neighboring mines reveal nearby safe squares automatically.
  • Avoid random clicking: Use visible information before taking a risk.
  • Watch your timer: Focus on accuracy first, then improve your completion time.

❓ Minesweeper FAQs

1. Is Minesweeper free to play online?

Yes. Play this browser-based Minesweeper game on Gameso for free without downloading a separate application.

2. How do you win Minesweeper?

Reveal every square that does not contain a mine. You do not have to flag every mine to win.

3. What do the numbers mean in Minesweeper?

Each number tells you how many mines are in the eight surrounding squares, including diagonal neighbors.

4. Is the first click always safe?

Yes. This version places mines after your first move and protects the first square and its neighboring squares.

5. How do I place a flag?

On desktop, right-click an unopened square. On a touchscreen, select Flag Mode and tap the square.

6. Can I play Minesweeper on my phone?

Yes. The game supports touch input and adjusts to smaller screens. You can scroll the board horizontally when necessary.

7. What are the Easy, Medium, and Hard settings?

Easy uses a 9 × 9 board with 10 mines, Medium uses a 16 × 16 board with 40 mines, and Hard uses a 30 × 16 board with 99 mines.

8. What happens when I click a mine?

The game ends in a loss, and the board reveals the mine locations.

9. What is the mine counter?

The counter starts with the total number of mines and decreases when you place flags. It does not verify whether each flag is correct.

10. Does Minesweeper improve logic skills?

Minesweeper encourages logical deduction, pattern recognition, careful observation, and planning.

11. Can I restart a Minesweeper game?

Yes. Select Restart to begin a fresh board at the same difficulty, or select New Game to start again.

12. Do I need to install anything to play?

No installation is needed. Open the game page in a modern browser and start playing.
