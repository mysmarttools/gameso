How to Play Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a classic puzzle game where you need to clear the board without clicking on a hidden mine.

Each revealed number tells you how many mines are located in the surrounding cells. Use these clues to find the safe cells.

Minesweeper Rules

  • 💣 Avoid all hidden mines.
  • 🔢 Numbers show nearby mines.
  • 🚩 Flag cells you believe contain mines.
  • 🏆 Clear every safe cell to win.
  • ⏱️ Try to complete the board as quickly as possible.
