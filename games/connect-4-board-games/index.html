Play Connect 4 Board Game Online

Enjoy this classic Connect 4 board game online for free on Gameso. Take turns with another player, drop your colored discs into the board and try to connect four discs horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

How to Play Connect 4

Connect 4 is a simple and fun two-player board game. Player 1 uses red discs and Player 2 uses yellow discs. Players take turns dropping one disc into any available column.

Connect 4 Board Game Rules

  • 🔴 Player 1 starts with red discs.
  • 🟡 Player 2 uses yellow discs.
  • 🎯 Connect four discs in a row to win.
  • ↔️ Horizontal connections count.
  • ↕️ Vertical connections count.
  • ↗️ Diagonal connections count.
  • 🤝 If the board fills without four in a row, the game ends in a draw.
