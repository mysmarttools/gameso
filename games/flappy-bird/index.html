What Is Flappy Bird?

Flappy Bird is a simple but challenging arcade-style game. You control a small bird that must fly through openings between rows of pipes. The bird falls because of gravity, so you need to tap or press a key at the right moment to keep it in the air.

This free Flappy Bird online game recreates the familiar one-button challenge with original canvas-drawn graphics. You can play directly in your browser without installing a game or creating an account. Your best score is saved locally in your browser when storage is available.

How to Play Flappy Bird Online

  1. Start the game: Click Start Game or tap the play area.
  2. Flap upward: Press Space, Arrow Up, click, or tap the game canvas.
  3. Avoid the pipes: Guide the bird through the open space between each upper and lower pipe.
  4. Stay in the air: Time each flap carefully so you do not hit a pipe or the ground.
  5. Earn points: Pass a pipe pair to earn one point.
  6. Beat your record: Continue playing and try to improve your best score.
💡 Pro tip: Avoid tapping too quickly. Use small, controlled flaps and watch the next gap before approaching the pipes. Consistent timing is more useful than frantic clicking.

Flappy Bird Controls

Keyboard Controls

Press Space or Arrow Up to make the bird flap. Press Enter to start a new run from the start screen or after a game ends. These keyboard controls make the game easy to play on desktop and laptop computers.

Mouse Controls

Click the game canvas to start playing or flap during a run. If the bird crashes, click again to begin another attempt.

Mobile and Touch Controls

On a phone or tablet, tap the game area to flap. The canvas scales to the available width while keeping the game proportions intact, so the bird and pipes remain visible on smaller screens.

Tips to Get a Higher Flappy Bird Score

  • Look ahead: Focus on the next opening instead of only watching the bird.
  • Keep a steady rhythm: Short, deliberate taps help you manage the bird's height.
  • Avoid overcorrecting: Too many rapid flaps can send the bird into the upper pipe.
  • Learn the movement: Practise how quickly the bird rises and falls after each input.
  • Stay calm: Concentration helps you judge gaps and react at the right time.
  • Practise regularly: Learn from each attempt and aim to beat your previous best.

❓ Flappy Bird FAQs

1. Is Flappy Bird free to play on Gameso?

Yes. This browser-based Flappy Bird game is free to play without a separate download or registration.

2. How do I make the bird fly?

Press Space, Arrow Up, click the game, or tap the canvas on a touchscreen device.

3. How do I score points?

You earn one point each time the bird successfully passes a pair of pipes.

4. What happens if the bird hits a pipe?

The run ends if the bird hits a pipe, the ground, or the top boundary. You can then restart and try again.

5. Can I play Flappy Bird on my mobile phone?

Yes. The responsive game supports touch controls on compatible mobile browsers and tablets.

6. Does Flappy Bird save my high score?

The game saves your best score in your browser's local storage when available. Scores are not shared between devices.

7. How do I restart Flappy Bird?

Use the Restart Game button, or press Enter after a run ends.

8. Can I mute the sound?

Yes. Use the Sound button to switch the game's synthesized sound effects on or off.

9. Why is Flappy Bird difficult?

The bird is constantly affected by gravity, so each flap must be timed carefully to pass through narrow pipe openings.

10. Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the page in a modern web browser and start playing.
