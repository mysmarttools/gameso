How to Play Flappy Bird

Control the bird and fly through the gaps between the pipes. Every pipe you pass gives you one point.

Click, tap the screen or press the Space key to make the bird fly upward. Avoid hitting the pipes and ground.

Flappy Bird Tips

  • 🐦 Tap gently to control the bird.
  • 🎯 Focus on the upcoming gap.
  • 🚫 Don't hit the pipes.
  • 🏆 Try to beat your high score.
