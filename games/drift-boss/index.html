Drift Boss Game

Play Drift Boss Game online and test your driving skills on an endless winding road. Control your car, master sharp turns, collect coins and try to achieve the highest score.

How to Play Drift Boss

Keep your car on the road for as long as possible. Use the drift controls to move around curves and avoid falling off the track. The longer you survive, the higher your score becomes.

Drift Boss Controls

  • Space: Hold to drift.
  • Left / Right Arrow: Steer the car.
  • R: Restart the game.
  • Mobile: Hold the Drift button.
