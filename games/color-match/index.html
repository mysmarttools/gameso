How to Play Color Match

Color Match is a fast-paced color recognition puzzle. Each round displays a target shade and a grid of colorful tiles. Your job is to find the exact match before the countdown reaches zero. Correct answers earn points, and each new round becomes more challenging.

  1. Start the game: Press Start Game to begin the first round.
  2. Study the target: Look at the large colored square above the grid.
  3. Choose a tile: Click or tap the tile that matches the target exactly.
  4. Beat the timer: Make your choice before time runs out.
  5. Keep scoring: Correct answers increase your score and advance the round.

Color Match Game Tips

  • Compare the target with every tile before making your choice.
  • Pay attention to small differences in brightness and shade.
  • Stay focused instead of clicking too quickly.
  • As rounds become harder, scan the entire grid before deciding.
  • Practise regularly to improve your speed and color recognition.
