About Crazy Guys

Crazy Guys is a fast-paced platform game built around jumping, movement and challenging obstacle courses. Your goal is to guide your character through each challenge, avoid falling and reach the finish line whenever the level requires it.

The action can get hectic, so good timing matters just as much as speed. Rushing through every obstacle may cause mistakes, while watching the course and choosing the right moment to jump can help you make progress.

Tips to Improve Your Game

👀 Look Ahead

Watch the next section of the course instead of focusing only on your character. This gives you more time to react.

🦘 Time Your Jumps

Avoid jumping too early. Learn the distance to each gap and jump when you have enough room to land safely.

🏃 Balance Speed and Control

Moving quickly is useful, but careful movement around narrow platforms can prevent unnecessary falls.

🔁 Learn from Each Attempt

If you miss an obstacle, try to identify what went wrong and adjust your timing on the next attempt.
