About Crazy Guys

Crazy Guys on this page features an original endless-runner experience created for Gameso. The game uses its own canvas-drawn track, player, obstacles, coins and interface. Nothing needs to be downloaded, and the gameplay runs directly in the browser.

Your character runs along a three-lane track. Barriers block the route, gaps in timing demand quick jumps, and coins reward accurate movement. The longer you survive, the faster the course becomes. Every run is a chance to improve your reaction time and beat your best score.

This is an arcade game built around simple controls and progressively harder challenges. It is easy to start, but staying on the track takes attention. Watch the obstacles, plan your next move and avoid making last-second changes when a barrier is close.

How to Play

  1. Press Start Game to begin your run.
  2. Move left or right to switch between the three lanes.
  3. Jump when a barrier is approaching your lane.
  4. Collect floating coins to increase your coin count.
  5. Avoid collisions and keep running to earn more points.
  6. When your run ends, choose Restart and try to beat your record.

The game gradually increases its speed, so a lane that looks safe now may have an obstacle approaching. Keep looking ahead and move early whenever possible.

Game Controls

Mobile Controls

On a phone or tablet, use the left, jump and right buttons shown at the bottom of the game screen. You can also tap the game to focus it. The game layout adjusts to smaller screens.

Tips for a Higher Score

👀 Look Ahead

Watch upcoming obstacles and choose a safe lane before reaching them.

↔️ Change Lanes Early

Move before the last moment. Early decisions give you more time to react.

🦘 Jump Carefully

Use jumps for barriers, but avoid jumping unnecessarily when a clear lane is available.

🪙 Collect Coins Safely

Coins are useful, but a risky coin is not worth ending a promising run.

⚡ Adapt to Speed

The game gets faster over time. Make smaller, more deliberate movements as speed rises.

🏁 Practice Consistently

Use each attempt to learn obstacle timing and gradually improve your score.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Crazy Guys game free to play?

Yes. The original browser game on this page can be played without purchasing an in-game upgrade. Internet access is needed to open the Gameso website.

How do I jump over barriers?

Press Space or the Up Arrow on a keyboard. On mobile, tap the Jump button. Time the jump so that your character is above the barrier as it reaches the player.

How do I collect coins?

Move into the lane containing a coin. Collecting a coin increases the counter displayed above the game.

Does the game get harder?

Yes. The running speed increases as your score grows, making obstacles arrive more quickly and requiring faster decisions.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game includes touch controls and a responsive canvas layout. For the best experience, use a modern mobile browser and hold the device in landscape orientation if the play area feels too narrow.

Does Crazy Guys save my best score?

The game attempts to save your best score in your browser's local storage. This works when browser storage is available and enabled. Clearing browser data may erase the saved score.

Why did my run end?

Hitting an obstacle ends the current run. Restart the game and try another lane or time your jump more carefully on your next attempt.
