How to Play Breakout

Control the paddle at the bottom of the screen and bounce the ball into the bricks. Break every brick to complete the level.

Don't let the ball fall below your paddle. You have 3 lives to complete the game.

Breakout Controls

  • ⬅️ Left Arrow — Move paddle left.
  • ➡️ Right Arrow — Move paddle right.
  • 🖱️ Move your mouse to control the paddle.
  • 📱 Drag your finger on mobile.

Tips

  • 🎯 Aim for the center of the bricks.
  • ⚡ Keep the ball moving.
  • ❤️ Save your lives.
  • 🏆 Clear every level to win.
