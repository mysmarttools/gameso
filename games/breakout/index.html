How to Play Breakout

Breakout is an arcade brick-breaking challenge where your paddle is your main tool. Send the ball into the brick wall, collect points, and prevent the ball from falling off the bottom of the screen.

  1. Press Start Game to begin your run.
  2. Move your paddle beneath the ball using your mouse, arrow keys, or your finger on a touchscreen.
  3. Press Space or tap the play area to launch the ball.
  4. Hit bricks to earn points. Clear all the bricks to advance to the next level.
  5. Keep the ball in play. You have three lives, so aim carefully and try to beat your best score.

Tips to Get a Higher Score

  • Watch the ball: Move early when it starts coming back toward the paddle.
  • Aim with the paddle: Contact near the paddle's edge changes the rebound angle.
  • Find openings: Aim for gaps in the brick formation to reach bricks above it.
  • Stay calm: Avoid sudden movements when the ball is moving quickly.
  • Plan your next level: Each new level increases the challenge.
Pro tip: You don't need to chase the ball at the last second. Predict where it will land and position the paddle before it gets there.

Breakout Game – FAQs

Can I play Breakout online for free?

Yes. This Gameso version runs directly in your browser and does not require a paid download or account.

How do I control the paddle?

Use the Left and Right arrow keys or A and D on a keyboard. On a computer, you can also move your mouse across the game area. On a touchscreen, drag your finger to guide the paddle.

How do I launch the ball?

Press Space or tap the game area after starting. The ball will launch upward from the paddle.

What happens when the ball falls?

You lose one life when the ball passes the paddle. The game gives you three lives at the start of each run.

How do I move to the next level?

Break every brick in the current formation. A new level will load with a different layout and a greater challenge.

Does the game save my high score?

The game attempts to save your best score in your browser's local storage. It may not persist if browser storage is disabled or cleared.

Can I play on a mobile phone?

Yes. The game layout adapts to smaller screens. Tap to launch the ball and drag across the play area to move the paddle.
