How to Play Pong

Control the left paddle and hit the ball back toward your opponent. The computer controls the right paddle.

The first player to score 7 points wins the match.

Controls

  • ⬆️ Use the Up Arrow to move up.
  • ⬇️ Use the Down Arrow to move down.
  • ⌨️ W and S keys also control your paddle.
  • 📱 On mobile, drag your finger vertically on the game.
