🎮 How to Play Pong

Pong is a classic arcade game where you control a paddle and compete against a computer opponent. Hit the ball back across the court and try to make the CPU miss. Every successful point brings you closer to victory.

Game Instructions

  1. Click Start Game to begin the match.
  2. Move your paddle up and down to hit the ball.
  3. Return the ball toward the computer's side.
  4. Score when the computer fails to return the ball.
  5. Reach 7 points before the CPU to win the match.

⌨️ Controls

  • Arrow Up / Arrow Down: Move your paddle.
  • W / S: Alternative paddle controls.
  • Mouse: Move the pointer over the game to position your paddle.
  • Space: Pause or resume the game.
  • Mobile: Use the on-screen buttons or drag your finger vertically over the game.

🏆 Tips to Win

  • Watch the ball carefully and move your paddle early.
  • Try hitting the ball near the top or bottom of your paddle to change its angle.
  • Stay near the middle when the ball is far away.
  • Predict where the ball will travel instead of chasing it at the last second.
