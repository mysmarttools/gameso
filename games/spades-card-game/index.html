How to Play Spades Card Game

Spades is a classic four-player partnership card game. Each player receives 13 cards and bids how many tricks they expect to win. Spades are always trump.

Spades Card Game Rules

  • 🃏 52 cards are dealt between four players.
  • 🎯 Players bid the number of tricks they expect to win.
  • ♠️ Spades are always the trump suit.
  • 🃏 You must follow the suit that was led if possible.
  • 🏆 The highest spade wins when a spade is played.
  • ⭐ First team to reach the target score wins.
