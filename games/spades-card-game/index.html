How to Play Spades

Spades is a trick-taking card game for four players. You and your North partner form one team, while West and East form the opposing team. Each player receives 13 cards from a standard 52-card deck.

1. Make a bid

Predict how many tricks you can win. Your bid combines with your partner's bid to make your team's target. A Nil bid means you aim to win zero tricks.

2. Follow suit

The first card played sets the suit for the trick. If you have a card in that suit, you must play it. If you do not, you may play another suit or use a Spade.

3. Spades are trump

The highest Spade wins when a Spade is played. Otherwise, the highest card in the suit led wins. Aces are high.

4. Score points

Meeting your team's bid earns 10 points per bid trick plus one point per extra trick. Missing your bid costs 10 points per bid trick. Extra tricks count as bags; every 10 bags results in a 100-point penalty in this version. A successful Nil earns 100 points; a failed Nil costs 100.

Helpful tips

  • Count your high cards and Spades before bidding.
  • Save powerful Spades for tricks that matter.
  • Follow suit whenever possible.
  • Watch your team's target and avoid unnecessary bags.

Spades Card Game FAQs

Is this Spades game free?

Yes. Play directly in your browser without downloading or installing anything.

Can I play against computer opponents?

Yes. Three computer-controlled players take part, including your North partner.

How many cards does each player get?

Each player gets 13 cards from a standard 52-card deck.

What does Nil mean?

Nil means you predict that you will win no tricks. This version awards 100 points for a successful Nil and deducts 100 for a failed Nil.

How can I win?

The first team to reach 500 points wins, if its score is higher than the opposing team's score at the end of the hand.
