How to Play Spades

Spades is a classic trick-taking card game for four players. You and your North partner play against West and East. The goal is to predict how many tricks your team can win and then meet that contract.

1. Bid before playing

Each player predicts the number of tricks they expect to win. Your bid and your partner's bid form your team's target. A bid of zero is called Nil: the player aims to win no tricks.

2. Follow suit

The first card played sets the suit for that trick. If you have a card of that suit, you must play it. If you do not have one, you can play another suit or use a Spade.

3. Spades are trump

If a Spade is played, the highest Spade usually wins the trick. If no Spade is played, the highest card in the suit that was led wins. Aces are high.

4. Break Spades

Normally, you cannot lead with a Spade until a player has used one to trump another suit. You can lead a Spade earlier if your remaining cards are all Spades.

5. Score points

If your team meets its bid, it earns 10 points for each trick bid, plus one point for each extra trick. Extra tricks are called bags. If your team misses its bid, it loses 10 points per trick bid. Collecting 10 bags causes a 100-point penalty in this version.

Beginner strategy tips

  • Count your Aces and high Spades before choosing a bid.
  • Follow suit whenever you can; the game enforces this rule.
  • Save high Spades for important tricks when possible.
  • Keep an eye on your team's target and avoid unnecessary extra tricks.

Spades Card Game FAQs

Is this Spades card game free to play?

Yes. You can play this browser-based version for free without downloading an app or creating an account.

Can I play Spades against the computer?

Yes. This version includes three computer-controlled opponents and a computer-controlled partner.

How many cards does each player get?

Each of the four players receives 13 cards from a standard 52-card deck.

What does Nil mean in Spades?

Nil is a bid of zero tricks. In this version, a successful Nil earns 100 points, while taking a trick after bidding Nil costs 100 points.

How does a team win?

The first team to reach 500 points wins, provided it has more points than the opposing team at the end of the hand.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the game in a modern browser on a desktop, tablet, or mobile device and play directly on the page.
