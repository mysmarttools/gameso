About This Maze Chase Game

Maze chase games combine navigation, timing and quick decisions. You move through connected corridors, collect items and try to avoid enemies that patrol or pursue you. A good route can help you gather more points while keeping an escape path open.

This Gameso page contains an original, self-contained browser maze game. The maze, character drawings, movement logic and enemy behaviour are implemented directly in this page. The game does not load a third-party game through an iframe.

Your goal is to clear the collectible dots while protecting your lives. Larger power pellets temporarily make the ghosts vulnerable, giving you an opportunity to move through dangerous corridors or earn bonus points. As you make progress, the challenge increases.

Game Features

  • A custom maze rendered on an HTML canvas.
  • Keyboard and on-screen directional controls.
  • Ghost enemies that navigate the maze and pursue the player.
  • Power pellets that temporarily change enemy behaviour.
  • Score tracking, lives, restart and pause controls.
  • A locally saved best score when browser storage is available.
  • Responsive layout for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

How to Play Maze Chase

  1. Press Start Game to begin moving through the maze.
  2. Use the arrow keys or WASD to navigate the corridors.
  3. Collect small dots to earn points.
  4. Collect large power pellets to temporarily make ghosts vulnerable.
  5. Avoid dangerous ghosts and use corners to change direction.
  6. Collect all remaining dots to complete the maze and progress.

Controls

⬆️⬇️ Up and Down Use the Up and Down arrow keys or W and S to move vertically.
⬅️➡️ Left and Right Use the Left and Right arrow keys or A and D to move through horizontal corridors.
⏸ Pause Click Pause or press P to pause or resume the game.

Tips for a Higher Score

  • Plan your route so you do not get trapped in a dead end.
  • Watch the ghosts before entering narrow corridors.
  • Save power pellets for areas where enemies are close.
  • Keep moving and change direction early when a route becomes dangerous.
  • Collect nearby dots before crossing a risky section of the maze.

Maze Chase – Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I play this maze game online for free?

Yes. The game runs directly in this Gameso page using HTML canvas and JavaScript. No separate game installation is required.

2. Is this the official PAC-MAN game?

No. This is an original maze chase game made for Gameso, inspired by the general maze arcade genre. It is not the official PAC-MAN release.

3. How do I control the player?

Use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard. On a mobile device, use the on-screen directional buttons.

4. How do I earn points?

Move through the maze to collect small dots and larger power pellets. Some power-pellet encounters can also reward bonus points.

5. What do power pellets do?

Power pellets temporarily make the ghosts vulnerable. During that time, you can catch them for bonus points instead of immediately losing a life on contact.

6. What happens when a ghost catches me?

You lose a life and return to a starting position. The game ends when you run out of lives.

7. Does the game save my best score?

It attempts to save your best score in your browser's local storage. Private browsing or clearing site data may remove the saved score.

8. Can I play on a mobile phone?

Yes. The page adapts to smaller screens and includes touch-friendly buttons for moving in all four directions.

9. Why is the game not loading?

Make sure JavaScript is enabled and use an updated browser. If the page was just deployed, wait for the hosting deployment to finish and reload the page.
