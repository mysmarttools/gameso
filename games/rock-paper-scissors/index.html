🎮 About Rock Paper Scissors

Rock Paper Scissors is a classic hand game that you can play in seconds. In this free online version, you compete against the computer by choosing one of three moves: rock, paper or scissors. The computer picks its move at random, and the game instantly reveals the result.

The rules are easy to learn. Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, and paper beats rock. If both sides choose the same move, the round is a tie. Play as many rounds as you like, track your score and try to win a short match or play without a round limit.

This browser game is designed for quick entertainment on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile devices. You do not need to download an app or create an account to play.

📖 Rock Paper Scissors Rules

Remember these three simple rules to decide the winner of each round.

✊ Rock beats Scissors

Rock breaks or crushes scissors.

✋ Paper beats Rock

Paper covers rock.

✌️ Scissors beat Paper

Scissors cut paper.

What Happens in a Tie?

When you and the computer select the same move, neither side wins the round. The score remains unchanged, and you can choose again immediately.

💡 Tips for Playing Better

  • Know the rules: Remember which move beats the other two.
  • Understand randomness: The computer's choices are random, so no move guarantees a win.
  • Play short matches: Use a win target to make each match more competitive.
  • Track your results: Check the scoreboard and recent-round history.
  • Enjoy the ties: A draw is part of the game and gives you another chance to win.
