How to Play Rock Paper Scissors

Rock Paper Scissors is a classic two-player game. In this online version, you play against the computer.

Choose one of the three options: Rock, Paper or Scissors. The computer will randomly select its move and the winner will be displayed instantly.

Rock Paper Scissors Rules

  • ✊ Rock beats Scissors.
  • ✋ Paper beats Rock.
  • ✌️ Scissors beats Paper.
  • 🤝 Matching choices result in a draw.
